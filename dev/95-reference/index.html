SmoothPeriodicHMM(families, a, θᴬ, θᴮ, T; basis_A)

A periodic HMM without memory whose transition probabilities and emission parameters are smooth functions of the period index. The observations are D × N, split into blocks of rows, one per family; given the state, the blocks are independent.

  • families: one per block, a distribution => degree pair (a seasonal_family with a trigonometric polynomial of that degree per parameter) or any TimeVaryingFamily. A univariate law covers one row; a multivariate law, built from scalar parameters, covers as many rows as its length.
  • init[k]: the starting parameters of state k, one per block (just the one when there is a single block): a number, a NamedTuple for a family with several parameters, or a coefficient vector. The states must start apart.
  • θᴬ, K × (K-1) × (2d+1): the transition coefficients, as for SmoothARPeriodicHMM, zero (uniform transitions) by default; a the initial distribution, uniform by default.
  • θᴮ[k, b]: the coefficient vector of block b in state k.
m0 = SmoothPeriodicHMM([Gamma => 1, Normal => 2], 2, 365;                       init = [[(α = 2.0, θ = 1.0), (μ = 0.0, σ = 1.0)],                               [(α = 1.0, θ = 5.0), (μ = 3.0, σ = 2.0)]])m, history = fit_mle(m0, Y; n2t, solver)       # Y is 2 × Nparameters(m).A                        # the transition coefficientsemission_coefs(m, 2, 1)                # state 2's Gamma coefficients, a writable viewcurve(m, :μ)                           # K × T, the Normal mean of each statePeriodicHMMDists(m)                    # the evaluated model

rand, viterbi, loglikelihood and loglikelihoods act as on PeriodicHMMDists(m). It is a HiddenMarkovModels.AbstractHMM whose controls are SeasonalControls(n2t).

SmoothSeasonalMixture(families, spec_α, nT, ps)

A mixture whose weights and component parameters are smooth functions of the period index. It holds one seasonal_family per component, the weights' parametrization spec_α, the period nT, and the coefficients ps.

  • SmoothSeasonalMixture(D, K, d, T; init, dα = d): K components of family D, each parameter a trigonometric polynomial of degree d, the weights of degree dα.
  • SmoothSeasonalMixture(pairs, T; init, dα = 1): one distribution => degree pair per component, so components may differ in family and degree.

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.ar1_spec — Method
ar1_spec(d, T) -> ParamSpec

The parametrization of a periodic AR(1): the three seasonal parameters of

y[n] = μ(t) + ρ(t) y[n-1] + σ(t) ε[n],   t = n2t[n]

as trigonometric polynomials of degree d over a period of T steps, each pushed through the link that keeps it in its domain — μ free, ρ ∈ (-1, 1) so that the process is stationary, σ > 0.

ParamSpec(    μ = TrigBasis(T, d) => IdentityLink(),    ρ = TrigBasis(T, d) => IntervalLink(-1, 1),    σ = TrigBasis(T, d) => LogLink(),)

The coefficients lie end to end in that order, 2d+1 of them each.

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.seasonal_family — Function
seasonal_family(D::Type, basis) -> TimeVaryingFamily

How the parameters of the distribution D vary with the period index: one basis and one link per parameter, in the order D's constructor takes them.

Exponential, Rayleigh, Gamma, Weibull, Normal, LogNormal, Bernoulli and Poisson are declared. Any other family becomes usable as a mixture component or an HMM emission with one method:

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.AR1 — Type
AR1(μ, ρ, σ)

A periodic AR(1) process given by its curves over one period:

\[y_n = \mu(t_n) + \rho(t_n)\, y_{n-1} + \sigma(t_n)\, \epsilon_n, \qquad \epsilon_n \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1),\]

where $t_n$ is the period index of step $n$. μ, ρ and σ are vectors of length T, or K × T matrices holding one process per hidden state, read as μ[k, t]. SmoothAR1 is the same process with the curves given by coefficients.

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.ARPeriodicHMM — Type
ARPeriodicHMM([a, ]A, B) -> ARPeriodicHMM

A periodic hidden Markov model with K states, D sites, period T and autoregressive emissions of memory order.

  • a: the initial state distribution, length K; uniform if omitted.
  • A: the K × K × T transition matrices; A[:, :, t] is the step leaving period index t.
  • B: the K × T × D × 2^order emission distributions: B[k, t, j, h] is the law of site j in state k at period index t, given the memory category h encoding the order previous values of that site.

ARPeriodicHMM(a, θᴬ, θᴮ, T; basis_A, basis_B) builds one from coefficients.

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.ARPeriodicHMM — Method
ARPeriodicHMM(a, θᴬ, θᴮ, T; basis_A, basis_B) -> ARPeriodicHMM

The ARPeriodicHMM of period T whose transitions and emissions are basis expansions over the period index t = 1:T, X[t, :] ⋅ θ with X = design_matrix(basis, 1:T):

  • θᴬ, K × (K-1) × nA: row k of A(t) is a softmax over X_A[t, :] ⋅ θᴬ[k, l, :], l = 1:K-1, with state K as the reference.
  • θᴮ, K × D × 2^order × nB: the success probability of site j in state k given memory category h is logistic(X_B[t, :] ⋅ θᴮ[k, j, h, :]).

The bases default to trigonometric polynomials, TrigBasis(T, d) for 2d + 1 coefficients. Any basis of TimeVaryingParameters works, for example CyclicBSplineBasis(T, n) for a periodic index, or BSplineBasis(1, T, n) for an index that is not periodic, such as a position.

SmoothPeriodicStatsModels.PeriodicHMMDists — Type
PeriodicHMMDists([a, ]A, B)

A periodic hidden Markov model with K states, period T and any emission laws, without memory.

  • a: the initial state distribution, length K; uniform if omitted.
  • A: the K × K × T transition matrices; A[:, :, t] is the step leaving period index t.
  • B: one K × T matrix of laws per block of rows, as a tuple (a single matrix for one block). B[b][k, t] is the law of the rows of block b in state k at period index t. A univariate law covers one row, a multivariate law as many rows as its length; the blocks follow one another down the rows.

Given the state, the blocks are independent: the emission is the product of their laws. It is the evaluated twin of SmoothPeriodicHMM, and a HiddenMarkovModels.AbstractHMM whose controls are SeasonalControls(n2t).
